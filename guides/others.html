    • Material Suppliers

    Places to get fur, minky, other fabrics, and fursuit specific parts have been covered by people way more qualified by me. This list is for everything else. Most useful if you're in the US.

    • Fursuit Materials (great list of materials)
    • Wawak has all sorts of sewing notions you may not be able to find elsewhere. They also have reasonable prices.
    • Foam Factory (get their Lux-HQ or Charcoal foam)
    • If you have a Microcenter nearby, their 3d printer filament is good quality and cheap. They also sell 3d printers if you're in the market for one.
    • K and J Magnetics is where I get magnets. They are strong.
    Communities

    These are communities that can help you with your fursuit projects.

    • Rat's Fursuit Making Lounge (discord): a general chat for makers and fursuit enthusiasts.
    • Fursuit Maker Dungeon (telegram): general chat for makers.
    • Fursuit Sales + Trades: telegram group for advertising fursuits for sale. Also has people selling bases and supplies
    • Faux Fur Marketplace (telegram): dm @covepalms on telegram to join. Group just for buying and selling faux fur.
    • Honestly? Go talk to non-furry creatives. I think that sometimes this fandom can get too closed off, and outside viewpoints can improve your work. Join an art club, talk to some cosplayers, hang out with the people at a sewing shop or makerspace.
    Makers I Love
    • CochayuyoCreations: I am obsessed with their suits. They make so many cool species and their stylization is great.
    • Spey Labs: A very distinct style! And blinking!!
    • Beastcub: Their work is so pretty! I love how they fur their heads, with airbrushing, long manes and feathers.
    • DragonArtist15: Their sculpts are amazing. I would love to make a skullie off a base of theirs.
    • Puzzled Jay Productions: They make so many pretty skullies and hard headed creatures.
    • itz._.shalom: I love the way they stylize their heads to look like they just came out of an anime show.
    • CityMutt Fursuits: Their stylization is peak.
