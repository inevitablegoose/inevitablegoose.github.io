Prices are for a medium complexity character of any species. Includes a custom sculpted headbase and all special features (eyelids, hair, horns, leds, fans or anything really). More complicated characters will cost more, and simpler characters will cost less.

How commissions for fursuits work:
  • Message me (Instagram, Discord: @inevitablegoose, Telegram: @inevitablegoot). Please put in your first message that you are interested in a commission.
  • I will review your character or concept, give a quote, select the commissions I'm interested in, and figure out details.
  • I will commit to 1-2 projects for every slot (a 3 month period). I usually commit to projects a slot in advance. I choose projects based on my personal preferences and interests.
  • Payment for materials is due when I start on a project. For the rest of the amount, either payment in full or a payment plan will be arranged.
  • Progress pictures and work updates are provided regularly, and feedback from the client is taken into consideration.
  • When the suit is finished and fully paid for, it is shipped out for you to enjoy!
